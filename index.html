I’m Shourya Sathwane, 18.

I’ve been working on projects I find exciting. One of the projects I became obsessed with, and carried forward to actual users, is Novaya.

Novaya works on context infrastructure for AI agents. The initial product, Novgraph, is a knowledge graph engine for codebases that integrates with Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, and more.

Novgraph changes the way coding agents work: surfacing co-change coupling, calculating the blast radius of a change, and downranking and flagging stale files.

One of the reasons I worked on knowledge graphs was that I was struggling to maintain my own codebase while working on an agent called Nexus.

Based on user feedback, I pivoted to Novgraph Sovereign, an on-device version that keeps the codebase and knowledge graph on the user’s device.
